# News

## v26.08 - 2026-08-11

### Highlights

- Super Bowling now delivers a complete hot-seat bowling experience for one to four players,
  with customizable balls, six rack sizes, generalized scoring, and local practice records.
- The lane has gained a more active arcade finish: a release-driven deck push, a richer rolling
  ball, dynamic fallen-pin presentation, and distinct strike and spare celebrations.
- Result moments now hold the final deck in view and offer a clear Continue action before the
  next aim, including a reduced-motion presentation.
- The game's visual direction is an original blend of screen bowling and arcade lane energy; see
  [LANE_MASTER_VIDEO_FINDINGS.md](LANE_MASTER_VIDEO_FINDINGS.md).
