## Completion boundary

The completed evidence supports readable ball travel through first impact while
the ball is renderable, a local collision neighborhood at impact, stable dense
decks under the measured production Canvas trace, monotonic normal camera
motion, neutral reduced motion, authoritative physics, and the settled-result
handoff. M10 is a concrete evidence blocker, permitted by this completion
boundary, for one unshipped behavior only: expanding the held view from
aggregate impulse alone. The current evidence cannot prove which later dense
contacts belong to the initial local cascade; tracking them needs a
physics-owned locality signal. The shipped behavior is deliberately limited to
the first authoritative ball-pin local hold, followed later by the authoritative
settled-result handoff. It does not invalidate the delivered local collision
view.
