# Accessibility

Super Bowling is tuned first as an energetic arcade bowling game: the release-driven camera
push, collision-led pin emphasis, earned strike and spare panels, and short particle bursts make
the outcome feel consequential. That normal presentation is the design and acceptance authority.

## Motion alternative contract

Reduced motion is a saved presentation preference, not a gameplay or visual-direction constraint.
Its lower-motion alternative must:

- preserve important game information, including the roll result, score, standing-pin state, and
  usable controls;
- provide a usable presentation with less movement; and
- keep the player's saved preference working across local sessions.

The preference adapts presentation after the normal arcade experience is authored. It does not
change scoring, physics, shot controls, or the information a player receives.

Normal-mode visual review judges camera movement, particles, pin-motion emphasis, celebrations,
and result staging on their own merits. The alternative is checked against the three guarantees
above; it does not set conservative limits for the normal presentation.
