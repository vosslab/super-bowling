# 1,000-pin action

The 1,000-mode rack contains 990 individually simulated pins. These six 1600 x 1000 captures
follow one full-power centered roll through the shipped browser build and real Rapier worker.
They are frames from one physical event, not a staged particle effect or a reduced pin fixture.

The quiet approach establishes the scale of the complete rack. At first contact the camera pushes
close enough that the near pin field fills the lane, while the broad triangle remains readable.
The moving region then expands through local pin-to-pin collisions rather than turning the rack
into a uniform explosion. The result view holds the deck long enough to compare the 557 fallen
pins with the 433 that remain standing.
