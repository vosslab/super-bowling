# Many-pin camera tie-break D

## Decision

Select `open`: 10% pin-height showing between adjacent rows, or 90% overlap.

## Original-resolution review

I independently reviewed the fresh 1600 x 1000 aiming captures at original resolution. `Open`
makes the individual depth rows legible while keeping the triangular rack as one believable
bowling rack.
The rear rows remain compact and the closer rows gain enough separation to explain why a
105-pin rack needs a more exaggerated faux-3D view than a regulation rack.

`Dense` reads as a flat picket fence. `Balanced` is less compressed, but still merges too many
rows. `Open` is the first candidate where the complete triangular rack is readable, cohesive,
and visually shippable without losing the lane's single vanishing direction.

The fixed 4% crown, 95% ball-bottom, and 91% occupied-span composition also uses the lane canvas
instead of recreating the large empty upper field seen in rejected layouts.
