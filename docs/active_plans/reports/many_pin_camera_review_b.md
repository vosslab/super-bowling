# Many-pin camera tie-break E

## Decision

Select `open`: 10% pin-height showing between adjacent rows, or 90% overlap.

## Original-resolution review

I independently reviewed the three fresh original-resolution 105-pin aiming images. `Open`
gives the rack the strongest useful depth rhythm: row crowns can be followed from the rear of the
triangle to the front, and the closer pins visibly advance without becoming detached objects.
It looks like an intentionally exaggerated bowling view rather than a flat front elevation.

`Dense` is a flat picket fence. `Balanced` remains compressed and leaves several rows merged.
`Open` makes the triangular rack readable and cohesive while preserving a continuous, plausible
lane; it is the only visually shippable candidate in this comparison.

The ball remains grounded at the near edge and the full rack stays inside the same vertical
composition. That stability is important when later shots remove pins.
